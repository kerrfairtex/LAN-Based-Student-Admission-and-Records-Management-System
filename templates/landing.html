About the system

What this system does.

This system manages student admission, enrollment, and academic records for TRAC Junior High School. The registrar's office and authorized data encoders use it to log applicant information, process admission decisions, assign enrollment for the active school year, and maintain student academic and transfer records. Access is restricted to registrar staff and authorized data encoders. This is an internal administrative tool, not a public admission portal. Admission decisions are finalized on campus through the registrar's office.

  • Records applicant information and admission requirements submitted to the registrar.
  • Maintains student profiles, enrollment history, and academic records (Form 138 / SF10 data).
  • Generates reports required by the school and by the Department of Education (Learner Information System).
  • Keeps an audit log of every sign-in, record change, export, and backup.
System functions

What the system does

The functions below are the actual administrative tasks the registrar's office performs in this system. Each corresponds to a section of the application used after sign-in.

Admission

Applicant & admission records

Record applicant information, review submitted documents, and process admission records. Includes an admission index, create/edit forms, and a printable applicant view.

Records

Student records

Maintain student profiles, edit personal and family information, view academic history, and print School Form 10 (SF10) records as needed.

Enrollment

Enrollment

Record enrollment for the active school year, assign students to sections, and track unenrolled or unassigned students.

Search

Records search

Search by student name, Learner Reference Number, or admission reference to retrieve an existing record quickly.

Reports

Reports

Generate admission status, enrollment summary, SF10, and student masterlist reports. Export Learner Information System (LIS) CSV files for DepEd reporting.

Audit

Audit log & backups

Review who changed what, and when. The registrar can export database backups and restore from a previous backup when needed.

Admissions

How to apply

1

Applicant Registration

Provide the applicant's basic information and contact details to the registrar's office, or submit them through the inquiry form at the bottom of this page.

2

Submit Requirements

Submit the required admission and supporting documents to the registrar's office on campus.

3

Admission Review

The registrar reviews the submitted information and requirements, and records the admission decision in the system.

4

Enrollment

Once admission is confirmed, the student is registered and enrollment information is recorded in the system.

Admission Requirements

Prepare the required documents before submitting your application or visiting the registrar's office.

  • PSA birth certificate (original + photocopy)
  • Form 138 / report card from previous school
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • 1×1 ID photos
  • Barangay residency certificate

Confirm the full list with the registrar's office before submission.

Frequently asked

Questions about admission & records

Quick answers to the things families and students most often ask the registrar's office. If you have a question that isn't here, the inquiry form below is the fastest way to reach us.

  • When does admission for the next school year open?

    The registrar's office begins accepting admission inquiries for the next school year shortly after the current school year ends. Submitting the inquiry form below is the first step; the registrar will follow up with the actual schedule and required documents.

  • What documents are required for admission?

    PSA birth certificate (original + photocopy), Form 138 / report card from the previous school, Certificate of Good Moral Character, two 1×1 ID photos, and a barangay residency certificate. The registrar will confirm the full list at the time of submission.

  • Is this system accessible from outside the school?

    No. The Student Admission and Records Management System operates inside the school's local area network and is not reachable from the public internet. Only authorized administrative computers on campus can sign in. This public page is the school's information site; the actual records system is private.

  • How can parents request a copy of Form 138 (SF10)?

    Request it through the registrar's office. The registrar verifies identity, locates the record in the system, and prints an officially endorsed Form 138. Please coordinate directly with the office for pickup or release.

  • Can I update my contact number or address after enrolment?

    Yes. Visit the registrar's office with a valid ID and request the update. The change is recorded in the student's profile and reflected in the school's enrollment and report generation.

  • Does the system generate DepEd reports automatically?

    Yes. Authorized staff can export the Learner Information System (LIS) CSV file and generate School Form 10 (SF10), enrollment summaries, and masterlist reports directly from the system.
